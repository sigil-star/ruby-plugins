# Avatar Frames

Animated rings around avatars everywhere in Ruby, colored by each person's status:

- **Online** — Emerald
- **In a game** — Ocean (with a soft glow)
- **In Studio** — Ember
- **Offline** — no ring

Your own avatar gets its own frame (Prismatic by default). Change any of them in
**Settings › General › Avatar Frames**: Ruby, Frost, Emerald, Royal Gold, Prismatic, Silver,
Ember, Ocean or none, plus animated/static and ring thickness. Honors Reduce motion.

Only adds CSS; it doesn't read or send any data.
